# D01 - Project Overview

**Purpose**: This document outlines the core business purpose, value proposition, key business scenarios, and critical technology considerations for the JMJ Financial Coaching static website. It focuses on the **why** and **what** from a business perspective, providing context for development decisions and feature prioritization.

## 1. Application Purpose & Value Proposition

A professional static website that helps individuals and families struggling with debt understand financial coaching services, builds trust through testimonials, and converts visitors into consultation bookings through multiple engagement channels (direct contact, Facebook Messenger, and Calendly scheduling).

**Value Proposition**: "Take people to financial freedom through personalized coaching and accountability, helping them change their behavior with money and achieve lasting financial peace."

## 2. Target Users & Their Goals

- **Debt-Struggling Individuals**: Find professional guidance to create a clear path out of debt and build sustainable financial habits
- **Families in Financial Crisis**: Get family-focused financial coaching to work together toward financial stability and peace
- **People Seeking Accountability**: Connect with a coach who provides ongoing support and accountability for financial behavior changes
- **Prospects Researching Services**: Understand what financial coaching is, how it works, and whether it's right for their situation

## 3. Key Business Scenarios

1. **Service Discovery & Education**: Visitor learns what financial coaching is, how it differs from other financial services, and how it can specifically help their debt situation
2. **Trust Building Through Social Proof**: Visitor reads testimonials and success stories from similar clients who achieved financial freedom
3. **Consultation Booking**: Visitor schedules a free consultation through Calendly integration or direct contact methods
4. **Facebook Messenger Engagement**: Visitor initiates conversation through Facebook Messenger for immediate questions or to start the coaching process
5. **Contact & Follow-up**: Visitor reaches out via email or phone to ask questions before booking a consultation

## 4. Technology Constraints & Preferences

- **Must Have**:

  - AstroJS static site generator
  - GitHub Pages hosting with automatic deployment on push to public branch
  - Calendly integration for appointment scheduling
  - Facebook Messenger integration for direct messaging
  - Google Analytics tracking
  - Mobile-responsive design

- **Preferred**:

  - Clean, professional design that builds trust
  - Fast loading times for better user experience
  - SEO optimization for local financial coaching searches

- **Constraints**:
  - Static site only (no server-side processing)
  - No user authentication or account management
  - Must work within GitHub Pages limitations
  - No mention of Ramsey coaching affiliation

## 5. High-Level Feature Categories

- **Service Information & Education**: Clear explanation of financial coaching services and benefits
- **Social Proof & Testimonials**: Client success stories and testimonials to build trust
- **Contact & Scheduling**: Multiple ways to book consultations (Calendly, email, phone, Facebook Messenger)
- **About & Credibility**: Information about Jennifer and her approach to financial coaching
- **Local SEO & Business Listings**: Optimization for local search and business directory presence
- **Analytics & Tracking**: Google Analytics integration and IndexNow for search engine updates
