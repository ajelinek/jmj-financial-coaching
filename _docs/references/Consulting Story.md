Let me tell you a little about myself and why I became a FC… My husband and I have been married for 12 years and we have 2 children. We always talked about me being a stay at home mom, but we didn't think it was financially possible, although we had no budget or written plan, that was just a general sense we had. Three years ago, I suddenly lost my job, and overnight our household income was cut in half. We were really scared. We put together a budget, and we made some lifestyle changes. Whereas we used to have money and no time, I found myself having more time and less money. That meant cooking at home more and eating out a lot less. I got really involved in my kids' school, and I got to be a stay at home mom! For the first time since they were born, I got to spend the whole summer with them, and I loved it. About two years ago, I found Dave Ramsey. His statistics really spoke to me. He said 78% of people live paycheck to paycheck and 66% of Americans would struggle to pay for a $1000 emergency. I remembered the fear we had felt a year earlier, and I decided I wanted to help other people take control of their money so they could find peace and reach their goals like we did. Becoming a financial coach has been a true blessing in my life.

## Transition to Recommendation

Thank you for being transparent. I really appreciate that. This is not easy stuff to talk about, but you've come to the right place. My job as a coach is to take you from where you are right now to where you want to go.

## Coaching Package Recommendation

Recommendation – X and Y, what I recommend for you is a 6-month coaching package that is designed to give you the financial habits you need to win with money. We will meet once a month via Zoom, and you will have email, text, and phone access as needed for questions. The first session we will go through all the numbers and create a budget. This budget will include EveryDollar to track your monthly expenses and a spreadsheet to track the entire year. This will make next year's planning so much simpler. One of the biggest reasons people hire me is because with your input, I will do the budget for you, and then hand it over to you. We will measure success by budget proficiency, how much debt is paid off, and emotional change of position.

Imagine a scale of 1-10 with 1 being hopeless and 10 being peaceful. My average client starts at a 2-4 (feeling overwhelmed) and almost all end between 7 and 9 (feeling confident). This is one client's story…

## Client Success Story

Testimonial – So my client by her own admission was making really good money, but she was always waiting for the other shoe to drop. She felt like she was on the cusp of having it all taken away. She had gotten behind on her mortgage payment, had medical bills go to collections, and taken out a payday loan for a car repair. She had tried budgeting but found that unexpected expenses always derailed her plans. By looking at her expenses together and talking through each category, we were able to identify some areas that could be reduced or eliminated. To reach her goals, she was willing to temporarily give up subscription services, a gym membership, and covered parking at work. She was also willing to cut way back on eating out, a huge problem area for her. The first month was a success just by not overspending her income, but by the second month she had an extra $600 to throw at the debt snowball each month.

Similarly, my average client pays off $5-10K of debt in the first 3-6 months.

## Accountability Component

Accountability – There is one more thing that I offer as a coach, and that is accountability. For some of my client's it's not a matter of knowledge, it's a matter of actually doing it. In the story I just told, the first month she spent her entire restaurant budget in the first week. I reached out to her and reminded her of the importance of her goals, and I'm happy to say, although it was a struggle, she didn't eat out again that month.

## Urgency Story

7 years ago, he had a client reach out for coaching, schedule a 30-minute consult, and never show up. 6 years later they called and asked him if he was still coaching. When they met with him, he asked them why it took 6 years to meet. The wife said we thought we'd save the money and do it ourselves. That didn't work out so well. The good news in this story is they paid off $10K of debt in the first 2 months of coaching. The unfortunate part is that the 6 years they tried to do it themselves cost them. If they had signed up 7 years ago when they first reached out, they would be debt free, money in savings, investing every month, and going on a vacation every year. They are working the plan, but they are still in debt today. Please don't be like this couple and wait 6 years to take control of your finances
